Elaheh Karooby

Nanophotonics | Integrated Photonics | Quantum Information Science

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at North Carolina State University. My research focuses on developing nanophotonic devices and photonic integrated circuits for quantum technologies.

I am particularly interested in using photonics to address challenges in quantum information science, from miniaturized cold-atom systems and quantum-light interfaces to programmable photonic processors for quantum computing.

Research Areas

Nanophotonics for Miniaturized Cold-Atom Systems and Quantum Sensing

GMOT gratings, metasurfaces, electromagnetic design, nanofabrication, optical integration, and experimental characterization.

Quantum Light & Nanophotonic Interfaces

High-Q nanophotonic cavities, quantum dots, efficient single-photon generation, and spin–photon interfaces.

Photonic Quantum Computing

Programmable photonic integrated circuits, experimental quantum signal processing, and hardware-native implementation of quantum algorithms.
