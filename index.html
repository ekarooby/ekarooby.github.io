PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at North Carolina State University

Her current research focuses on the design and fabrication of photonic devices, including optical gratings and multifunctional metasurfaces for miniaturized GMOT systems, as well as efficient spin-photon interfaces for quantum networking and computing. Elaheh’s current work integrates computational techniques, such as Genetic Algorithms, RCWA, and FDTD software, to improve device performance and robustness. She is passionate about advancing quantum-photonic devices towards measurement-based quantum computing.!
