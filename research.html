Research

My research focuses on developing nanophotonic devices and photonic integrated circuits for quantum technologies. I combine electromagnetic design, numerical optimization, nanofabrication, and experimental characterization to address challenges in quantum information science.

Nanophotonics for Cold Atoms & Quantum Sensing

I develop nanophotonic devices, such as gratings and metasurfaces, for miniaturized magneto-optical trap systems. My work includes electromagnetic design, inverse design and optimization, nanofabrication, optical-system integration, and experimental characterization of nanophotonic devices.

Quantum Light & Nanophotonic Interfaces

I design nanophotonic structures for efficient light-matter interfaces, including non-periodic high-Q cavities as single-photon emitters. My work couples Lumerical API and genetic algorithm to improve optical confinement and photon collection for single-photon generation and spin-photon interfacing.

Photonic Quantum Computing

I work on implementation of quantum algorithms on programmable photonic integrated circuits (PICs). My recent work includes the experimental realization of quantum signal processing using a dual-rail single-photon qubit on a programmable PIC for nonlinear function synthesis.
