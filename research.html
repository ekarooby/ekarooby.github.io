Research

My research focuses on developing nanophotonic devices and photonic integrated circuits for quantum technologies. I combine electromagnetic design, numerical optimization, nanofabrication, and experimental characterization to address challenges in quantum information science.

Nanophotonics for Miniaturized Cold-Atom Systems and Quantum Sensing

I develop nanophotonic structures for compact magneto-optical trap systems, including high-efficiency two-dimensional diffraction gratings and multifunctional metasurfaces. My work includes electromagnetic design, optimization, nanofabrication, optical-system integration, and experimental characterization of grating efficiency and polarization performance.

Quantum Light & Nanophotonic Interfaces

I design nanophotonic structures for efficient light-matter interfaces, including non-periodic high-Q cavities for quantum emitters. My work uses electromagnetic simulation and genetic-algorithm optimization to improve optical confinement and photon collection for single-photon generation and spin-photon interfacing.

Photonic Quantum Computing

I work on implementing quantum algorithms directly on programmable photonic integrated circuits. My recent work includes the experimental realization of quantum signal processing using a dual-rail single-photon qubit, with programmable interferometric transformations used for hardware-native nonlinear function synthesis.
